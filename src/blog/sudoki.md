## The Idea

Sudoki was born from a simple frustration: most Sudoku apps are cluttered with ads, lack meaningful competition, and feel like isolated experiences. I wanted to create something that brought back the joy of solving puzzles while adding a social layer that makes each day's puzzle feel like a shared event.

## Key Features

- **Daily Puzzle** - A new, handcrafted puzzle every day
- **Global Leaderboards** - See how you stack up against players around the world
- **Clean Interface** - No ads, no clutter - just pure Sudoku
- **Progress Tracking** - Build streaks and track your improvement over time, whether offline or signed in

## Design Philosophy

The interface is intentionally minimal, but fun. A blend of soft, playful and low-contrast colors ensure comfortable play in any environment, while the responsive design means you can tackle the daily puzzle whether you're at your desk or on the go.
